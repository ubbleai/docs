# Create a business applicant

To create a business applicant, call the [Create a business applicant](#tag/Business-applicants/operation/create_business_applicant) endpoint.

Optionally, provide the following fields:

- `external_applicant_id` – Your identifier for this applicant

Make a note of the business applicant `id` value in the response. You'll need it to create verifications later.

Once you have created a Business Applicant, you can start creating verifications as described in the sections below.
You can then [retrieve](#tag/Business-applicants/operation/retrieve_business_applicant) the profile.
