# Website Verification (Coming soon)

The Website Verification solution enables you to verify the compliance of a business' website and extract relevant information from this website. This helps meet compliance requirements and reduce the risk of fraud.

The solution works as follows:
- Create a verification linked to the Business Applicant's profile.
- We check whether the website is compliant with your policy and extract the relevant data.
- Verify webhook notifications and retrieve the verification to see the result.
